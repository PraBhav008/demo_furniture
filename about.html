Made by hand, in the open.

Aurelia Verde began at a single kiln and a borrowed workbench. It has grown, but not by much, on purpose.

Our story

We started with a simple frustration: homes full of objects nobody loved and nobody could repair. So we set out to make fewer, better things with people we could look in the eye.

Today the studio is a small team of ceramicists, weavers and metalworkers who still sign their work.

Philosophy

Slow production, honest materials and a finish that improves with use.

Vision

A home in which every object has a story you can tell, and a maker you can name.

The craft

  1. Sketch

    Forms are drawn and tested in clay or wood before anything is committed.

  2. Make

    Pieces are thrown, woven or cast in small runs by one maker from start to finish.

  3. Finish

    Glazes are fired, wool washed, brass polished, each by hand.

  4. Inspect

    Anything that falls short of our standard stays in the studio.

What we value

Care

For materials, makers and the people who use what we make.

Longevity

Objects should outlast trends and be repairable.

Honesty

Clear about where things come from and what they cost to make.
